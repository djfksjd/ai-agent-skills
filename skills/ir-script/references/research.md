### R03 · Y Combinator, A Guide to Demo Day Presentations · US
- URL: https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/guide-to-demo-day-pitches/
- Publisher/date: Y Combinator, Geoff Ralston, 2016-07-25.
- Access: **full_text** of article; investor guidance, not a company deck.
- Observed: audience and a few remembered points guide the story. The organization section allows variation, including strong traction near the beginning. The guide discusses clarity, concise slides and rehearsal.
- Applied rule (our synthesis): select an order based on stage and evidence before making slides; do not impose the same numbered outline on every company. A short live pitch seeks a useful next conversation, not a complete diligence file.

### R04 · Sequoia, Writing a Business Plan · US
- URL: https://sequoiacap.com/article/writing-a-business-plan/
- Publisher: Team Sequoia. Original page date not shown in inspected text.
- Access: **full_text**; observed section from Company purpose through Vision.
- Observed sequence in this guide: purpose, problem, solution, timing, market, alternatives, model, team, financials, vision.
- Applied rule: use these as business-coverage questions, not proof of one universal slide count. Explain the buyer and competing alternatives, not just a feature catalogue. This is a guide, not a reconstruction of Airbnb's actual presentation.

### R05 · Front founder's Series A retrospective · US
- URL: https://front.com/blog/inside-fronts-series-a-slide-deck
- Publisher/date: Front, Mathilde Collin; dated 2016-08-02, with later retrospective text on the page.
- Access: **full_text** of article, particularly 'investors’ feedback'; embedded Slideshare was opened but its full visual sequence was not analyzed.
- Observed: founder highlights capital efficiency and customer expansion as strengths; acquisition scalability and projections were weaknesses; buyer and usage evidence needed more attention.
- Applied rule: a product demo alone is not an investment case. Add the buyer, acquisition path, retention/expansion and explicit use of capital when relevant. Historical company metrics do not become benchmarks or the user's traction.

### R06 · Buffer, seed-deck retrospective · US
- URL: https://buffer.com/resources/the-slide-deck-we-used-to-raise-half-a-million-dollars/
- Publisher/date: Buffer, Leo Widrich, 2013-05-23.
- Access: **full_text** of article, especially traction and competition sections. Not a full visual slide audit.
- Observed: founder describes traction as an important early fundraising argument and competition questions as a recurring source of confusion.
- Applied rule: decide whether verified traction belongs early, and explain position within the actual alternatives. Never remove real competitor capabilities. This article reports one company's experience, not a causal guarantee of fundraising success.

- Publisher/period: NAVER; 2025 Q4 results. Retrieved via the company's IR library; exact release day not established here.

### R08 · Microsoft FY25 Q4 prepared remarks and Q&A · US
- URL: https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/investor/events/fy-2025/earnings-fy-2025-q4
- Publisher/date: Microsoft Investor Relations, 2025-07-30.

### R11 · Front Series B founder retrospective · US
- URL: https://front.com/blog/front-series-b-deck
- Publisher/date: Front / Mathilde Collin, displayed 2018-03-19.
- Access: article text; embedded Slideshare not visually inspected.
- Locators: 'Process', 'Feedback I received', 'Tactical advice'.
- Observed: the founder discusses milestones and resource needs, usage/cohort evidence, efficient growth, and weaknesses in selling price, self-service adoption, churn and customer additions.
- Adaptation: connect product value to actual customer behavior and acquisition evidence; prepare answers to weak metrics. Do not present founder feedback as independent causal proof or reconstruct unseen slide order.

### R12 · Front Series C founder retrospective · US
- URL: https://front.com/blog/front-series-c-deck
- Publisher/date: Front / Mathilde Collin, displayed 2022-02-24; this is not asserted as the pitch date.
- Access: article text; embedded deck not visually inspected.
- Locators: 'The process', 'Feedback I’ve received'.
- Observed: prior milestones, new milestones and needed resources frame the fundraising explanation; selected slide numbers appear in feedback, not a complete chronological transcript.
