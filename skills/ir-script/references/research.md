# Research playbook and inspected reference register

Research date: **2026-09-28**. These are historical examples and current documentation inspected on that date, not continuously maintained facts. Recheck volatile conditions in each project. This register is original analysis with links, not redistributed decks or transcripts.

## Research procedure

1. Read the brief and existing files. Establish audience, stage, presentation goal and time. Distinguish a live investor pitch, competition judging, pilot proposal and public-company earnings update.
2. Start from supplied links. Search exact title/video ID, publisher's official event page, official captions, public deck and speaker's own explanation. Log failures and coverage. Do not derive a timeline from a video thumbnail or a search snippet.
3. Expand with primary material in Korea and abroad matched to the case. Suggested search patterns: `[company] investor presentation pdf site:[official domain]`, `[event] 데모데이 발표 공식`, `[founder] fundraising deck`, `[company] earnings transcript`.
4. Extract a reference map: observed segment, source locator, audience question, evidence used, visual/spoken device, transferable principle and what does NOT transfer. Do not copy another company's claims or assume its funding success was caused by the deck.
5. Research the user's own market separately: buyer counts, customer workflow, competing products/prices, usage policies, data and unit economics. IR examples establish presentation patterns, not evidence for the user's business.
6. Synthesize two candidate narratives. Choose with a reason. Cite concrete source facts; label your structural recommendation as synthesis.
